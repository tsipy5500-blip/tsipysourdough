Accessibility statement

Draft for verification. Before publication, the marked details must be completed and the wording checked with an accessibility specialist or another professional.

Having trouble accessing the site? You can contact me on WhatsApp, by phone or by email, and I will sort it out.

I want everyone to be able to use this site. It was built with accessibility in mind from the start and is designed according to the Israeli standard SI 5568 at level AA, which is based on the WCAG guidelines. The business is an exempt dealer (osek patur), so there is no legal obligation to meet the standard on the website, but I chose to do so anyway.

What has been done on the site

  • At the top of every page there is an “Accessibility options” button: larger text, high contrast, underlined links, a readable font and stopping animations. The settings are saved in your browser only.
  • You can navigate the whole site using the keyboard alone. There is a “Skip to main content” link at the top of every page, and keyboard focus is clearly marked.
  • The page structure includes organized headings and marked regions, to make things easier for screen readers.
  • Every meaningful image has alternative text. Customer messages also appear as text.
  • The site is written in Hebrew, right to left, and the page language is declared. Translated versions in English, French and Spanish are also available, with the language declared on each page.
  • The contrast between text and background was checked, and you can enlarge the text using the buttons at the top of the page or using your browser, up to 200% without breaking the layout.
  • The site is adapted for mobile, and motion on the site is kept to a minimum and respects your device's “reduce motion” setting.
  • The site has no forms and no videos. Ordering and registration are done on WhatsApp.
  • In the image carousels you can browse with the keyboard (arrow keys) and with the “Previous” and “Next” buttons, and there is no automatic movement.

Known limitations

  • Orders and registrations are made in the WhatsApp app, which is not under my control.
  • An initial internal check was carried out, automatic and partly manual. A check by an accessibility specialist has not yet been carried out, so some defects may remain.

Service accessibility arrangements

Orders and registrations are made on WhatsApp. Anyone who finds it difficult to use WhatsApp is welcome to contact me by phone or email.

To confirm with Tsipy: the accessibility of the pickup location and the workshop venue, so that the accessibility arrangements of the service and the place can be published here.

Ran into a problem, or need an accommodation?

I'd be glad to hear from you and fix it. The accessibility contact is Tsipy Peretz. You can reach me on WhatsApp or by phone: 051-5775319, or by email: Tsipy5500@gmail.com.

I will try to reply quickly and fix the reported defect, and in the meantime offer an alternative way to receive the service.
