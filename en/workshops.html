Boutique sourdough workshop

Looking for a moment for yourself, connection, and the real taste of home? Come for a special experience of learning and enjoyment with sourdough bread made only from whole-grain flours and natural nutrition that feels good in the body.

150 ₪per participant
small, intimate groups

What happens in the workshop?

  • You'll learn to make real sourdough bread, simply and without complications.
  • Each participant leaves with a personal dough ready to bake at home.
  • Working only with whole-grain flours: spelt and whole wheat.
  • Indulgent tastings of natural spreads and fresh breads.
  • An introduction to the world of natural nutrition in an accessible and tasty way.
  • You'll receive a full recipe booklet and support after the workshop.

What a workshop looks like

From the preparation, through the kneading and the doughs, to the tasting table. You can scroll sideways.

How does it work?

We meet on a relaxed, fun morning (or evening) at my place: we learn, knead, taste and enjoy together. It's not just a baking workshop, it's an experience of connecting to yourself, to real food and to simplicity.

And the starter itself?

A sourdough starter is a living mixture of flour and water that ferments the dough without commercial yeast. When it is active it bubbles and grows after feeding, and then it is ready for bread.
